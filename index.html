about me

I'm an incoming 2026-27 IRiSS predoctoral researcher at Stanford, in the Language and Cognition Lab with Dr. Mike Frank. I currently teach English in Changhua, Taiwan on a Fulbright ETA grant. I graduated from UCLA in 2025 with a BA in Applied Linguistics. At UCLA, I was a research assistant in the Speech and Cognitive Development Lab (now also at Stanford!) in the Department of Linguistics. I have extensive experience working with typically hearing children and children with hearing loss across cultural contexts, both in the US and Taiwan. My research interests include spoken language acquisition and everything to do with cochlear implants.
