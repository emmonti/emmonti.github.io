about me

I'm an incoming 2026-27 IRiSS predoctoral researcher at Stanford, in the Language and Cognition Lab. I graduated from UCLA in 2025 with a BA in Applied Linguistics, where I was a research assistant in the Speech and Cognitive Development Lab. I have extensive experience working with typically hearing children and children with hearing loss across cultural contexts, both in the US and Taiwan. My research interests include phonological acquisition in children with hearing loss, wearable technology & naturalistic data collection, and everything to do with cochlear implants. I hope to pursue a Doctor of Audiology and PhD and continue to work with children in both clinical and research environments.
